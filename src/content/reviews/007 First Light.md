IOI surprises us with an amazing linear action game that is the lovechild of both Uncharted and Hitman, my favorite 2 game franchises.

I have played Hitman World of Assassination trilogy a LOT at this point, it is truly one of the best stealth sandbox games ever made (play it please if you love stealth games). I was thus excited for anything IOI would make, and a James Bond game sounded amazing with their espionage experience.

After playing this game, I am genuinely surprised how good the action set pieces and story was. If you have played Hitman WOA, you know the story was a disappointing part of that game. While intriguing us at first, it wraps up quickly in Hitman 3 and ends with a disappointing mission (if you wanna call it that, a linear train mission just for the story). Luckily the story wasn't a priority as Hitman WOA is made for replaying the maps again and again to remember it back to front.

<summary>Spoilers: Story</summary>
For 007 First Light, story and action gameplay was its main priority, so IOI really needed to surprise us with those. I am glad to tell you they absolutely nailed it! Story is intriguing and sits well with the world we live in today, with AI being the main focus of the game and how it has affected spycraft and transfer of information.
